Project Details

Overview

Built to streamline online shopping for customers and reduce friction in product discovery, cart updates, and checkout. The project demonstrates how thoughtful UI structure and interaction design improve conversion and usability, while the modular layout makes future expansion easy for new categories, payment options, and personalization features.

In the future, this platform can grow into a full storefront with inventory sync, recommendation engines, and analytics-driven merchandising. It is different from many template e-commerce clones because it emphasizes clear UX flow, reusable components, and a performance-first layout rather than relying on heavy frameworks.
